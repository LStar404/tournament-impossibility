# Actual ordered block construction

`Carrier m k` is a nested finite sum of the first k block index types.
`matrix m A k` is the actual ordered join, with +1 across every earlier-to-later
block and −1 in the reverse direction. It is not an abstract moment packet.

The module proves its cardinality and skew-symmetry, and identifies its first
two all-ones source energies with the corresponding explicit cumulative
polynomial fields. The third/sixth-moment field is proved in the separate
`OrdinalSixthMatrixBridge` module. All k and empty blocks are allowed.
