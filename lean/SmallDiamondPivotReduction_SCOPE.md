# Actual small-diamond source reduction

For n≥4 and the genuine diamond count D satisfying 0<D and 24D≤n², the theorem
constructs an actual source switch, with genuine directed-cycle cardinality
c>0, nc≤12D, and 2c≤n. It also proves that the sixth Frobenius moment is
unchanged. There are no assumed cycle-count, link-average, or moment identities.

This is a graph reduction, not yet the final small-diamond sixth-moment gap.
